Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next?

Every protocol below is priced from the snapshot's own traded tokens: a fitted curve says what its token would be worth if it launched, the peers' record at its age says how often launches happen, and the product of the two is its implied value. No protocol is hand-picked, and no multiple is set by hand.
